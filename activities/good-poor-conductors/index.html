First, Let's Understand

Imagine heat as tiny travellers. Some materials give these travellers an easy path, while others make the journey much slower.

Watch the Heat Travel!

METAL → FAST
PLASTIC → SLOW
WOOD → VERY SLOW
Notice the moving yellow particles!
In metal, heat travels quickly. In plastic and wood, heat travels much more slowly.
